# Privacy Policy for ConsignTracker

**Last updated: March 17, 2026**

## Overview

ConsignTracker ("the App") is operated by DWC Stuff ("we", "us", or "our").
This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account information**: Email address and password when you create an account,
  or your Apple ID name/email if you sign in with Apple
- **Inventory data**: Item names, descriptions, prices, photos, locations,
  categories, and sale status that you enter into the app
- **Location names**: Store and consignment location names you save in the app

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device information**: iOS version, device type, and app version for
  crash reporting and compatibility
- **Usage data**: Basic app usage patterns to improve the app experience

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect precise GPS location data
- We do not track you across other apps or websites
- We do not sell your data to third parties

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide and sync your inventory data across your devices
- To authenticate your identity and secure your account
- To send push notifications you have opted into (e.g., reminders)
- To improve app performance and fix bugs

## Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using **Supabase**, a cloud database provider.
All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Your account is
protected by secure authentication tokens stored in your device's Keychain.

Photos you add to items are stored in Supabase Storage and are private to
your account.

## Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

- **Supabase** (supabase.com) — database, authentication, and file storage.
  [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **Apple** — Sign in with Apple authentication, push notifications,
  and in-app purchases. [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- **Apple MapKit** — for displaying store location maps within the app.
  Map data is not sent to our servers.

## Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your
account at any time from the app's Settings screen, which permanently removes
all your data from our servers.

## Children's Privacy

ConsignTracker is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data (available within the app)
- Correct inaccurate data (editable within the app)
- Delete your data (via "Delete Account" in Settings)
- Export your data (via "Export Settings" in Settings)

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant
changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
